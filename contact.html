Common Questions

Can I request an early check-in or late check-out?

Yes — please message us at least 48 hours in advance. We accommodate when the schedule allows, usually at no additional charge.

Are pets welcome?

Pets are considered on a case-by-case basis. Message us before booking with details about your pet and we'll let you know.

Is the property suitable for families with young children?

Absolutely. The bunk room and private fenced yard make it ideal for families. The neighborhood is safe, walkable, and wonderful for kids.

What's the best way to get restaurant recommendations?

Browse our La Jolla Dining Guide — it's curated specifically for Village Pearl guests, with walk times from the property and host favorites marked.

Can I book directly without using Airbnb or VRBO?

Reservations are currently processed through Airbnb or VRBO to ensure guest and host protections are in place. Both platforms offer secure payment, verified reviews, and host guarantees. Direct booking options may be available in the future — feel free to ask.
