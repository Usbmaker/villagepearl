Experience the Timeless Aura

Step into a sanctuary where historic grandeur meets the effortless ease of coastal living. The Village Pearl is a meticulously preserved Craftsman cottage that does more than house guests — it captures a feeling.

White-painted hardwood floors, sheer linen curtains, a stone fireplace, and sliding doors that open directly onto the deck — every detail is curated for the kind of rest you only find at the coast.
