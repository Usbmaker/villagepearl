The Conrad

La Jolla Music Society · Baker-Baum Concert Hall

11 min walk ♥ Host Pick

7600 Fay Ave, La Jolla  ·  (858) 459-3728  ·  theconrad.org

Opened in 2019, The Conrad Prebys Performing Arts Center is the permanent home of the La Jolla Music Society — one of the most respected presenting organizations in the country. The Baker-Baum Concert Hall seats 1,000 and has been called a "lovely jewel box" by Wynton Marsalis and "one of the most beautiful halls we have played" by the Israel Philharmonic.

The programming is international in scope and relentlessly excellent — chamber music, jazz, world music, and solo recitals by performers at the very top of their fields. Checking the season calendar before your visit is highly recommended.

The Acoustic Story

The hall's acoustics were designed by Yasuhisa Toyota of Nagata Acoustics — the same acoustic engineer behind the Walt Disney Concert Hall in Los Angeles, Tokyo's Suntory Hall, and Hamburg's Elbphilharmonie. Every material, surface angle, and air handler in the building was chosen for sound. The result is extraordinary.

"One of the best acoustic halls in the country — in the world." — Critics and performers, consistently

La Jolla Playhouse

Est. 1947 · UCSD Campus

20 min drive

2910 La Jolla Village Dr, La Jolla  ·  (858) 550-1010  ·  lajollaplayhouse.org

Founded in 1947 by Gregory Peck, Dorothy McGuire, and Mel Ferrer, La Jolla Playhouse is one of the most decorated regional theaters in American history. Since its revival in 1983, it has sent 37 productions to Broadway, accumulated 42 Tony Awards, and mounted 132 world premieres. Jersey Boys, The Who's Tommy, and Big River all originated here. Winner of the Tony Award for Outstanding Regional Theatre.

"The most exciting theater in America." — repeatedly cited in national theater criticism

MCASD

Museum of Contemporary Art · La Jolla

12 min walk

700 Prospect St, La Jolla  ·  (858) 454-3541  ·  mcasd.org

San Diego's foremost contemporary art museum, with a collection spanning every medium from 1950 to the present. The La Jolla location sits on Prospect Street above the Cove — the Edwards Garden Gallery offers sweeping views of the Pacific alongside the art. Thoughtfully curated, never overwhelming, and consistently surprising.
