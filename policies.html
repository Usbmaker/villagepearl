House Policies

We want every guest to feel at home. These guidelines protect the property, respect the neighborhood, and ensure a seamless experience for everyone who stays at the Pearl.

House Rules

🚭

No Smoking

Strictly non-smoking indoors and on all decks. Designated outdoor area available. A deep-cleaning fee of $400 will be applied for violations.

🎉

No Parties or Events

The Village Pearl is a residential home in a quiet neighborhood. Gatherings beyond the registered guest count are not permitted.

🌙

Quiet Hours

10:00 PM – 8:00 AM. Please be mindful of neighbors on the upper deck and in the backyard during evening hours.

🐾

Pets

Pets are considered on a case-by-case basis with prior written approval. A pet fee applies. Please inquire before booking.

🔑

Lost Keys / Lockout

A $75 fee applies for lost keys. After-hours lockout assistance is available but may incur an additional service charge.

🧹

Condition at Departure

Please leave the property in the condition you found it. Dishes washed, trash secured in outdoor bins, and all doors locked. Professional cleaning is included — excessive mess may incur an additional fee.

🚗

Parking

Street parking is available in the surrounding neighborhood. La Jolla Village is a walkable area — once you've parked, most of your activities can be reached on foot. Guests are responsible for any parking citations or violations.

📅

Cancellation Policy

The Village Pearl's cancellation policy is managed through Airbnb or VRBO and is displayed at the time of booking. Please review the applicable policy on the platform you use to book before confirming your reservation.

🛡️

Damage & Liability

Guests are responsible for any damage to the property, fixtures, or furnishings beyond normal wear and tear. Airbnb bookings include AirCover host protection; VRBO bookings include VRBO's comparable guest and host guarantee. Please report any accidental damage promptly — honesty is always appreciated and often means a more favorable resolution for everyone.

Questions about our policies? We're happy to help before you book.
